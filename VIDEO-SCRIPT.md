Video script — bullet points, ~2:30, silent + captions

Cold open (0:00–0:18)

Homepage, cursor idle → caption: "Before you wire money to a counterparty — check the public record."
Cursor drifts to search field → caption: "Sanctions lists. Legal entity registration. VAT status."
Type Apple Inc. into the field, letter by letter → caption: "One search. Three independent registries."
Click "Verify"

Results page (0:18–0:58)

Panels appear with loading skeletons, then resolve → caption: "Live queries — nothing cached, nothing stored."
Hover the LEI panel (lift animation visible) → caption: "Legal Entity Identifier — pulled straight from GLEIF."
Highlight the "Source: GLEIF · as of…" footer → caption: "Every fact is sourced. Every fact is dated."
Move to VAT panel, show the valid SAP record → caption: "VAT validity — live from the EU's VIES system."
Move to Sanctions panel, showing a clean "No match found" → caption: "No matches here — clearly marked, not just a blank space."

The flagged case (0:58–1:22)

Cut to a second search that returns a real public sanctions-list entry → caption: "When a name is on a public list, it's flagged as a possible match — not a verdict."
Click into the match card, remarks expand, confidence score visible → caption: "You see the confidence score and the source. You make the call."
Hover "Copy matched name" briefly

Batch mode (1:22–1:54)

Cut to homepage, click "Batch verification" → caption: "Need to check more than one entity?"
Click "Load sample data," then "Run batch check" → caption: "Paste a list. Check up to 50 at once."
Results table populates, click "Flagged" filter, expand a row → caption: "Filter by status. Expand any row for the full detail."
Click "Export CSV" → caption: "Export straight to CSV for your file."

Close (1:54–2:15)

Cut back to the calm homepage hero → caption: "No risk scores. No black-box verdicts."
Hold → caption: "Just the sourced facts — so your team can form its own judgment."
Logo mark centered, fades → caption: "Entity Check"

A few production notes carried over:

Slow, deliberate cursor movement — reads as "expensive" to this audience, fast whip-pans don't.
Record in dark (ink) theme throughout — it holds up better in LinkedIn's autoplay thumbnail.
No caption on screen for less than ~4 seconds or more than ~10 — keeps pacing even with no voiceover to carry it.
